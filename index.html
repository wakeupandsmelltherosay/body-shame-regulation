What state are you in?

Which of these feels
most like you right now?

🔥Fight / Flight
Some of us go into fight-or-flight:
  • obsessive mirror checking
  • comparison spirals
  • an urge to eat less or start a diet
  • compulsive exercise
  • counting calories, researching cosmetic procedures, or searching for a way to “fix” your body as quickly as possible
🕯️Shutdown / Collapse
Others go into a freeze state:
  • feeling numb
  • disconnected
  • bingeing or over-eating
  • checking out of your body
  • avoiding people
💔Overwhelm
Or you may get overwhelmed:
  • everything feels “out-of-control”
  • you get shaky, teary, or panicked
  • you don’t know how to make the feelings stop

Regulating your nervous system helps your brain access a more accurate, compassionate view of your body. These practices create the conditions for that.
